Dialbubble vs Quo (formerly OpenPhone)

Quo is a complete business phone service, and it's the better choice for most teams: it has a shared inbox, Android and real in-app calling. Dialbubble is a narrower tool for one person who wants to use their own Twilio account from an iPhone, with no monthly plan.

Choose Quo if

  • More than one person answers the phone or the texts.
  • You need Android, a desktop app or in-app calling over Wi-Fi.
  • Incoming calls and texts must reach you immediately.
  • You make more than a few hundred call minutes a month.
  • You want AI call summaries or an AI agent to answer calls.

Choose Dialbubble if

  • You already have a Twilio account, or want pay-per-use with no plan.
  • You make few calls, and work on iPhone alone.
  • You want Twilio features such as spoken message calls, audio and custom TwiML.
  • You run several Twilio accounts or numbers and don't want per-number fees from an app.
  • You want your calls and messages kept only in your own Twilio account.

How much would each cost one person?

Quo's price stays flat, while Twilio's grows with use. A Talk call bills two legs, which is why the gap widens quickly for frequent callers.

Why choose a Quo alternative at all?

Mostly because of how you pay and who holds the account. With Dialbubble there's no plan to outgrow, and your numbers stay at Twilio, where you can also use them with code later. The trade-off is real: no in-app calling and no alerts when the app is closed.

If you're deciding between phone services and not committed to Twilio, compare Dialpad and Grasshopper too, or start from our roundup of business phone apps for iPhone.

What both have to do for US texting

Neither app gets you out of registration. US carriers require it for business texting from a local number, so you'll register with Quo or, on Twilio, through A2P 10DLC registration. Quo says its fee is $19.50 once. For a Sole Proprietor, Twilio's one-time fees also add up to $19.50.

Common questions

Is Quo the same as OpenPhone?
Yes. OpenPhone now goes by Quo. Its site reads "Quo (formerly OpenPhone)", and the company's legal name is still OpenPhone Technologies, Inc.
Is there a free trial of Quo?
Quo describes a 7-day free trial. Texting isn't included, because your number has to be registered first, and neither are international calls and texts.
Can Dialbubble do what Quo's shared inbox does?
No. Dialbubble is built for one person on one iPhone, with no shared workspace, assignments or team notes. A team should use Quo or something like it.
Can I use my Twilio number with Quo?
They're separate services. You'd have to port the number to Quo, which then becomes your provider. Quo says it supports switching from another provider and offers guidance on the transfer.
Which is cheaper for one person?
It depends on minutes. Light users pay less on Twilio with Dialbubble. Once you pass roughly 400 to 500 Talk minutes a month, Quo's flat plan usually costs less.
