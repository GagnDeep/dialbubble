Dialbubble vs the Twilio Console

They aren't really rivals: Dialbubble needs a Twilio account, and you'll still use the Console to buy numbers, register for texting and change settings. The Console is the official, complete control panel. Dialbubble covers the everyday calling, texting and checking you'd do from your phone.

Choose the Twilio Console if

  • You're buying, porting or configuring phone numbers.
  • You're registering for A2P 10DLC or a regulatory bundle.
  • You need to add funds, or see invoices and payment methods.
  • You're managing teammates and their roles.
  • You use products Dialbubble doesn't touch, such as Studio, Verify or Flex.

Reach for Dialbubble if

  • You want to place a call without opening a laptop.
  • You want to read and answer texts as conversations.
  • You want a fast look at your balance, usage and logs on your phone.
  • You want an error explained in plain English.
  • You switch between several accounts during the day.

A sensible way to use both

Do the setup at a desk: buy a number, set where calls go, register for texting, then create an API Key for your phone. After that, use the app for daily work, and return to the Console for changes. Your Console logs remain the record of everything.

Not sure about the credentials part? Read Auth Token vs API Key, then see how Dialbubble stores them. An API Key can be revoked in the Console if you lose your phone.

Twilio's role descriptions are worth a look before you hand anyone full credentials, as the Console can give them narrower access than an app can. Need a verified number for caller ID? Add it in the Console.

Common questions

Can Dialbubble replace the Twilio Console?
No. You still need the Console to create your account, add funds, buy numbers, set where incoming calls go and register for A2P 10DLC.
Does it cost anything to use the Console?
No. The Console comes with your Twilio account. You pay for what you use, the same as when you use Dialbubble.
Is Dialbubble a Twilio product?
No. Dialbubble is independent and uses Twilio's public API. Twilio doesn't make, endorse or support it, so for help with your account, go to Twilio.
Does using Dialbubble change what Twilio charges?
No. There's no markup. Calls and texts made in Dialbubble appear in your Console logs and on your Twilio bill as usual.
