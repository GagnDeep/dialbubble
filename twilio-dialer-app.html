A Twilio dialer app for iPhone

A Twilio dialer lets you place calls from your Twilio number without writing code. Dialbubble does this on iPhone in four ways, from a live two-way call to a call that just delivers a message. It isn't a VoIP softphone, so a live call uses your regular phone line.

Which call mode should you use?

Talk

A real conversation from your business number.

Twilio rings your phone, you press any key, and only then is the other person dialed.

Who gets rung
You, then them
What Twilio bills
Two legs, about $0.028 a minute in the US

Voice Message

A reminder or update that should arrive as a call.

You type the message. Twilio calls them and reads it aloud with text-to-speech, and can repeat it.

Who gets rung
Only them
What Twilio bills
One leg, about $0.014 a minute in the US

Play Audio

A recorded announcement in a real voice.

Twilio calls them and plays an MP3 or WAV from a public link.

Who gets rung
Only them
What Twilio bills
One leg, about $0.014 a minute in the US

Custom TwiML

Anything else Twilio's call language can do, with no webhook server.

Twilio calls them and runs the TwiML you wrote in the app.

Who gets rung
Only them, unless your TwiML dials more
What Twilio bills
One leg, plus whatever your TwiML adds

Local rates from Twilio's US voice pricing, November 2026. International rates vary by country, so use the calculator.

How do you send a voice message call?

Good for appointment reminders, a delayed delivery or a note to someone who doesn't read texts.

Type what it should say, choose a voice and how many times to repeat it, and pick the number to call from. You aren't on the line. Afterward, the call appears in Recents with its status and duration.

If you want more control, such as a menu or a redirect, write it as TwiML. Our TwiML examples and TwiML builder help you get started.

Caller ID and call history

Each call can use a different outgoing number: any Twilio number you own, or a verified caller ID you've added. New to this? Read what outbound caller ID is and how to call from a different number.

Recents shows your account's calls with filters, and each call has a detail page with its status and any recording. Here's how recording works.

Where this dialer stops

It places calls and doesn't answer them. It has no calling queue for working through a list, and live calls need your phone to be able to receive a normal call. If you need any of those, a business phone app will likely suit you better.

Twilio dialer questions

What number does the other person see?
The Twilio number you pick for that call, or a verified caller ID from your account. They don't see your personal number.
Is Dialbubble an auto dialer or power dialer?
No. It places one call at a time when you tap, with no dial lists, no CRM link and no browser extension. For a sales calling queue, look at a dedicated sales dialer.
Can I hang up from the app?
Yes. During a call, the app shows its status and can end it. If you don't press a key on a Talk call, it's cancelled and the other person is never dialed.
Can I record calls?
Yes. Turn on recording for a call, then play it back or share it from the app. Twilio charges for recording and storage, and consent rules depend on where you and the other person are.
Can I start a call by voice?
Yes. Siri and Shortcuts can call with a voice message or open the dialer.
