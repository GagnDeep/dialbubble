A Twilio SMS app for your iPhone

To read and answer Twilio texts on a phone, you need a third-party app, since Twilio doesn't publish a general one. Dialbubble is a free option: a threaded inbox with templates, scheduling and broadcast. Its big limit is no push notifications, so it fits best if you check in on your messages, not if you must see each one right away.

Answering a customer from your phone

Threads are grouped by your Twilio number and the other person, so you can see which of your numbers each conversation belongs to. Each message shows its delivery status, and the app shows how many segments a message will use before you send it.

  • Long messages and emoji can use more segments. Test yours with the SMS segment calculator.
  • Pictures you receive open in the thread. To send one, paste a public https link, because the app has no server to upload to.
  • Save your common replies as templates and drop them in with a tap.

Working through several numbers

If you run more than one number or Twilio account, the inbox keeps them apart with nicknames and colors. Global search finds anything across messages, calls and numbers.

  • Set a default sending number for each account.
  • Switch between Twilio accounts from Home.
  • This is an inbox for one person. There's no shared team inbox, assignment or notes.

Sending to many people, or later

Scheduled texts

Choose a time and Twilio sends it, even with your phone off. You can cancel it later. It needs a Twilio Messaging Service. Here's how to schedule SMS with Twilio.

Broadcast

Send one message to many people, with throttled sending and a result for each. Twilio bills every segment to every recipient, so check the cost first. For regular campaigns with opt-out handling and reporting, a dedicated SMS marketing tool is the better choice.

What does Twilio SMS cost?

In the US, November 2026, Twilio lists $0.0083 per SMS segment each way, plus carrier fees of roughly $0.0035 to $0.005 a segment. Picture messages sent are $0.022 plus carrier fees. A local number is $1.15 a month.

Sending from a US local number also means A2P 10DLC fees. For a Sole Proprietor, Twilio lists $4.50 for the brand, $15 for campaign vetting and $2 a month for the campaign. Other business types pay differently, so try the A2P 10DLC cost calculator or follow our registration guide.

Dialbubble itself is free and adds nothing to these prices.

When this isn't the right SMS app

Look elsewhere if you need any of these:

  • Instant alerts for each incoming text
  • A team working from the same inbox. Quo is built for that
  • Automated replies or opt-out handling built around your workflows
  • Android

Texts not arriving? Start with fixing error 30007 or error 21610, or read how Twilio SMS works.

Twilio SMS app questions

Will I get a notification when a text comes in?
Not instantly. Dialbubble has no server, so there are no push notifications. It checks every 20 seconds while it's open, and you can turn on an icon badge and best-effort alerts while it's closed, which iOS runs through Background App Refresh. If you need instant alerts, this is the wrong app for you.
Do I need A2P 10DLC registration to use it?
If you text US numbers from a regular local number, yes. That's a US carrier and Twilio rule, not something the app adds. Without it, messages can be blocked.
Why was my message not delivered?
The app shows Twilio's error in plain English. A common one is 30007, where carriers filter the message. Our guides explain how to fix it, along with 21610, which means the person opted out.
Can I schedule texts without a server?
Yes. Twilio holds scheduled messages, so they go out even if your phone is off. It requires a Twilio Messaging Service, whose SID you add in Settings.
Can I send a broadcast to a list?
Yes, with throttled sending, progress and a result for each person. Only message people who've agreed to hear from you. Marketing rules apply, and you're responsible for following them.
