What Twilio charges for

We don't list rates here because they change and they're Twilio's to set. This is what you can expect to see on your bill.

A phone number
A monthly fee for each number you keep.
Texts
Charged per message segment. A long text or one with emoji can use more than one segment, and Dialbubble's segment counter shows you how many before you send.
Picture messages (MMS)
Charged per message, separately from SMS.
Talk calls
Two legs: one call to your phone and one to the other person. Twilio bills both.
Voice Message, Play Audio and Custom TwiML calls
Normally one leg, to the other person. Custom TwiML can add more, depending on what you write.
Call recording
Twilio charges for recording and for storing recordings, if you turn it on.
Number lookups
Basic validation is included in Twilio Lookup. The line-type and caller-name add-ons are paid.
US texting registration
Texting US numbers from a regular local number requires A2P 10DLC registration, which has its own Twilio and carrier fees.

Why Talk calls cost more than you might expect

A Talk call rings your regular phone through Twilio, so you pay for two calls rather than one. Your mobile carrier may also treat the call to you as a normal incoming call under your plan. How two-way calls work

Pricing questions

Will Dialbubble ever charge me anything?
Dialbubble is free today, with no in-app purchases and no subscription. If that ever changes, we'll say so plainly on this page and in the app before anything happens.
Do you add a markup to Twilio's prices?
No. Dialbubble doesn't handle your usage or your payments at all. Twilio bills your account directly.
How do I keep an eye on what I'm spending?
The Home dashboard shows your balance, and the usage view shows monthly usage and cost by category, straight from your Twilio account.
Can I try it without paying anything?
Yes. Demo Mode needs no Twilio account and costs nothing. Twilio also offers trial accounts, which can only reach verified numbers and don't allow two-way Talk calls or custom text. Real day-to-day use needs an upgraded Twilio account.
