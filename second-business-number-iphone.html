Second phone number for business on iPhone

You have three good ways to do it: a second eSIM line from a carrier, a business phone app, or your own Twilio number used through an app like Dialbubble. Most people should pick one of the first two, as they ring your phone and show texts as they arrive. Here's how they compare.

A second eSIM line

Best if you want it to behave exactly like a phone line.

Apple's Dual SIM gives you two numbers with normal calls and texts, built into the Phone and Messages apps. iPhone 13 and later can hold two eSIMs. You pay for a second carrier line, and only one line can use cellular data at a time.

A business phone app

Best for most small businesses.

Grasshopper starts at $14 a month billed annually, Quo at $15 per user and paid Google Voice at $10 per user (November 2026). You get in-app calling and voicemail on Wi-Fi or data. Compare business phone apps.

Your own Twilio number

Best at low volume, or if you already use Twilio.

No plan: you pay for the number and what you use, about $7.28 a month for 100 call minutes and 100 texts. It takes more setup, has no in-app calling, and Dialbubble can't alert you to new texts when it's closed.

Estimate from Twilio's US list prices, November 2026: one local number at $1.15 a month, Talk calls billed as two legs at $0.014 a minute each, texts sent at $0.0083 plus up to $0.005 in carrier fees, and a $2.00 monthly Sole Proprietor campaign. It leaves out incoming calls and texts, recording, taxes and the one-time $19.50 registration. Sources: voice, SMS, A2P 10DLC fees. Try your own numbers in the Twilio pricing calculator.

How to set up a Twilio number as your second line

This takes more steps than the other routes, and the third step is the one people miss.

  1. 1

    Get a Twilio account and number

    Sign up and buy a number in the Twilio Console. A US local number is $1.15 a month as of November 2026.
  2. 2

    Connect the number to your iPhone

    Enter your Twilio keys in Dialbubble, choose the number as your default, and enter your regular number for Talk calls.
  3. 3

    Forward incoming calls

    Dialbubble doesn't answer calls, so point the number at your regular phone or voicemail in the Twilio Console. Your personal number stays private, as callers only see the Twilio number.
  4. 4

    Register for US texting

    If you'll text US numbers from a local number, complete A2P 10DLC registration. This has its own fees and takes time for approval.

More detail: what a Twilio number costs, how to forward calls and the A2P 10DLC registration guide. Numbers outside the US may need a regulatory bundle.

What happens when a customer calls or texts?

This is where the three routes differ the most, and it's worth checking before you print the number on anything.

Calls with an eSIM or a phone app
Your iPhone rings on that number. You answer as you'd expect.
Calls to a Twilio number
Twilio does what you told it to. Forwarding to your regular phone bills an inbound and an outbound leg, and Dialbubble isn't involved.
Texts to a Twilio number
They wait in your account. Dialbubble shows them when it's open, checking every 20 seconds, with no push when it's closed. So a customer's urgent text could wait until you next open the app.

Second number questions

What's the easiest way to get a second number on iPhone?
For most people, a business phone app or a second eSIM line. Either rings your iPhone directly and shows new texts straight away, with little setup. A Twilio number takes more work and suits you if you want its pay-per-use pricing or already have an account.
Can I have two numbers on one iPhone with no app?
Yes, with Dual SIM. Apple says you can use one number for business and another for personal calls. iPhone 13 and later can use two eSIMs. Your carrier must support it, and you'll pay for a second line.
Is a second number free?
Google Voice offers a free consumer number to eligible US residents, with Google's limits on inactive numbers. Otherwise, expect to pay a monthly fee to a carrier, an app or Twilio.
Will callers see my personal number?
Not when you call through the second number. With Dialbubble, the other person sees your Twilio number. A forwarded incoming call also doesn't show them your personal number.
