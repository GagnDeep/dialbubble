How to send SMS from iPhone with Twilio

You can send SMS from an iPhone with Twilio in three ways: the Twilio Console in Safari, an iPhone Shortcut that calls Twilio's API, or an app connected to your account. All three need an upgraded Twilio account, and none of them goes through the Messages app, because your Twilio number isn't on your SIM.

Each one makes the same request to Twilio, and Twilio's prices are the same however you send. If you'd like the background, read how Twilio SMS works.

What do you need first?

  • An upgraded account. On a trial, Twilio only sends predefined templates to verified numbers, so you can't type your own message. See Twilio's trial limits.
  • A number that's allowed to text. To reach US phones from a local number, you need A2P 10DLC registration, or you'll get error 30034. A toll-free number needs its own verification. Texting from an iPhone doesn't change this, since carriers treat all Twilio traffic the same way.
  • Numbers in the right format. Twilio wants E.164 format, such as +12025550123.

Option 1: How to send from the Console in Safari

This takes no setup, so it's the best way to check that your account can send at all.

  1. Open the Twilio Console in Safari and sign in.
  2. Find the Messaging section and its Try it out or Send an SMS page. Menus differ between Console versions, and the site is built for bigger screens, so you may need to zoom.
  3. Enter the recipient, choose your number as the sender, and type the message.
  4. Send it, then check the status in the Messaging logs.

Option 2: How to build an iPhone Shortcut that texts through Twilio

Apple's Shortcuts app can call web APIs, and it's already on your iPhone. This version asks what to say, then sends it to one fixed person, which suits something like texting a client or your team. It's also a useful example of how simple the API is.

  1. Create an API key. In the Twilio Console, create a Standard API key and copy its SID (starts with SK) and secret. An API key is safer here than your Auth Token, because you can revoke it on its own.
  2. Build the login text. In Shortcuts, tap + and add a Text action containing your key SID, a colon, then your secret, with no spaces. Add a Base64 Encode action after it. If it offers a line breaks option, choose None.
  3. Ask what to send. Add Ask for Input with the prompt "Message".
  4. Call Twilio. Add Get Contents of URL and expand its options. Fill it in as shown below, using your own Account SID in the URL, which starts with AC. Insert the two results as variables where the square brackets are.
  5. Run it and read the result. Add Show Result at the end. A working send returns details including "status": "queued". An error returns a code you can look up in our Twilio error guide.

To send at a later time, a Shortcut can add MessagingServiceSid, ScheduleType and SendAt. Our guide on how to schedule SMS with Twilio has the rules.

Option 3: Using an app

If you text regularly, you'll want an inbox rather than a one-way Shortcut. Check three things before you give any app your credentials: where it stores them, whether it runs them through its own server, and whether it adds a fee on top of Twilio's rates.

Dialbubble, the app made by this site, is built for this. It's a free iPhone app that talks directly to Twilio, keeps your credentials in the iPhone Keychain, and adds no markup. It covers a text inbox, MMS, templates, scheduled texts and Shortcuts. It has limits too:

  • There's no instant push for incoming texts. It checks every 20 seconds while it's open, and optional alerts while it's closed run when iOS allows a background check.
  • MMS needs a public https link to the image, as it doesn't host files for you.
  • Scheduled texts need a Messaging Service.
  • It's iPhone only, and it can't get around the trial limits or registration above.

Can you send SMS from an iPhone with Twilio for free?

Only in the trial, and only with templates. Everything else is pay-as-you-go. As of November 2026, Twilio's US pricing is $0.0083 per segment plus a carrier fee, and a US local number is $1.15 a month. Say you send 200 one-segment texts in a month, all to Verizon customers, whose carrier fee is $0.005:

  • Texts: 200 x ($0.0083 + $0.005) = $2.66
  • Number: $1.15
  • Total: $3.81, plus your registration fees

An emoji or a long message can raise that by splitting a text into more segments. The SMS segment calculator shows the count before you send.

Questions

Can I send a Twilio text from the iPhone Messages app?
No. The Messages app sends through your SIM or iMessage. A Twilio number lives in your Twilio account, so another app or a web request has to ask Twilio to send the text.
Can I send SMS from my iPhone with Twilio for free?
Not in any lasting way. The 30-day trial covers 100 SMS, but only predefined templates to verified numbers. Your own wording needs an upgraded account, and Twilio then charges per segment.
Is it safe to put Twilio credentials in a Shortcut?
It's stored in the Shortcut as plain text, so use an API key rather than your Auth Token, and never share that Shortcut. If it's ever exposed, revoke the key in the Console.
How do I see replies on my iPhone?
Replies stay in Twilio until something reads them. You can check the Console logs, or use an app that reads them from your account. Dialbubble checks every 20 seconds while open and doesn't send push notifications for texts.
