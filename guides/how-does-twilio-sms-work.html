How does Twilio SMS work? Step by step

Twilio SMS works by turning an API request into a text: you send Twilio the number, sender and message, Twilio queues it and hands it to a carrier, and the carrier delivers it to the phone. Along the way the message moves through statuses you can check, and any reply comes back to your Twilio number.

The short version

Twilio sits between your software and the phone networks. You don't connect to AT&T or Verizon yourself. You make one HTTPS request to Twilio, and it deals with routing, splitting long messages and collecting delivery receipts.

You need three things before the first real text goes out:

  • Credentials. An Account SID with an Auth Token or API key. Our guide to finding your Account SID and Auth Token shows where they are.
  • A sender. A Twilio number or a Messaging Service with numbers in it.
  • Permission to send. For US traffic that means A2P 10DLC registration on a local number, or verification on a toll-free one.

Where the text goes

  1. Twilio checks the request. Problems it can spot immediately, such as a badly formatted number (error 21211), come back as an error rather than a message.
  2. Twilio encodes and splits the text. It picks the most compact encoding and divides long messages into segments. Our guide to GSM-7 and UCS-2 encoding shows how that changes the count.
  3. The message waits in a queue. A US long code sends one segment per second by default. A text can wait for up to 10 hours. After that it fails with error 30001.
  4. Twilio hands it to the next carrier. When that carrier accepts it, the status becomes sent.
  5. Carriers carry it to the phone, or block it. The carrier can run spam filters here, which is where error 30007, message filtered comes from.
  6. A delivery receipt comes back. The status becomes delivered or undelivered.

What each status means

How replies reach you

A Twilio number has no inbox of its own, so nothing rings or pops up when someone replies. Twilio logs the text as an inbound message with the status received. Then one of these happens:

  • You set a webhook. Twilio sends the message to your URL, and your server answers with TwiML to say what to do next. An empty <Response> receives the text without replying.
  • You read it later. The message sits in your logs, where the Console, the API or an app can read it.

Some replies never get that far. On long codes, Twilio handles opt-out words such as STOP on its own by default. Once someone opts out, your next text to them is blocked with error 21610.

Is Twilio SMS free?

No, apart from the trial. The trial includes 100 SMS, but those can only be predefined templates sent to verified numbers, so you can't type your own text until you upgrade. Our page on Twilio's free trial limits has the details.

After that you pay per segment. As of November 2026, Twilio's US SMS pricing is $0.0083 per segment, plus a carrier fee on outbound long-code texts:

A message ending in failed costs $0.001 as of November 2026, under Twilio's failed-message fee, with exemptions for Twilio's own errors. undelivered is different: there's no failed fee, but Twilio charges its normal fees when delivery was attempted. So a filtered text usually still costs money. To estimate a whole month, try the Twilio pricing calculator.

Rules your text has to pass

The technical path is the easy part. Most new accounts get stuck on one of these:

  • Trial limits. Sending to an unverified number gives error 21608.
  • Registration. A US local number without an approved campaign gets error 30034.
  • Length. The body is capped at 1,600 characters (error 21617), and Twilio suggests staying under 320. The SMS segment calculator shows the count before you send.

Delivered isn't always delivered

Twilio's filtering article notes that, in rare cases, carriers report filtered messages as delivered. If someone says they got nothing, test with a different message before you blame their phone.

Questions

Is Twilio SMS free?
No. The 30-day trial includes 100 SMS, but only predefined template messages to verified numbers. After you upgrade, you pay per segment plus a carrier fee, and a monthly fee for the number.
Does 'delivered' mean the person read my text?
No. It means Twilio got confirmation of delivery from the carrier and, where available, the handset. SMS has no read receipts. The read status only applies to RCS and WhatsApp.
Why does my message stay on 'sent' and never reach 'delivered'?
Sent only means the next carrier accepted the text. If that carrier never returns a delivery receipt, Twilio has nothing further to report, so the status can stay on sent.
Do I need to write code to use Twilio SMS?
Not for simple sending. The Console has a page for sending a test SMS, and apps and iPhone Shortcuts can make the API call for you. Receiving automated replies does need a webhook or a TwiML Bin.
