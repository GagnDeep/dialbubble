Twilio error 21703: No sender in the service

Twilio error 21703 means your Messaging Service has senders, but none can send this message to this recipient. Add a sender suited to that country and message type, or finish its registration.

What does Twilio error 21703 mean?

Twilio's name for it is The Messaging Service does not have a phone number available to send a message.

Twilio accepts the request, then looks through your Sender Pool for a sender that can do the job. If it finds none, the message fails, so this shows up after the send, not as an immediate rejection.

If you use scheduled texts, this matters a lot, because scheduling requires a Messaging Service. A service with the wrong senders fails later, at send time.

Dialbubble shows "Scheduling problem" and reminds you that scheduled texts need a Messaging Service and a time 15 minutes to 35 days ahead.

Most likely causes, in the order to check them

  1. You're texting a US or Canadian mobile, and the service has no US or Canadian number or short code.
  2. The service only has a short code, and it can't reach this recipient.
  3. You're sending MMS without a US or Canadian long code or an MMS-enabled short code.
  4. The service only has an alphanumeric sender ID, which the country doesn't support.
  5. The 10DLC sender isn't tied to a verified A2P campaign.
  6. You just added the sender, and registration is still pending.

How to fix Twilio error 21703

  1. Open the Messaging Service and look at its Sender Pool.
  2. Add a number that fits the destination country and message type.
  3. For US recipients over 10DLC, register your brand and campaign, then add the numbers to the service. See the A2P 10DLC guide.
  4. If you just added a sender, wait, or send temporarily with an explicit From naming a sender that works.
  5. If you use a sender ID abroad, add a phone number as a fallback for countries that don't support it.

How to prevent it

  • Test a real send to each country and message type before relying on a service.
  • Keep one number from each region you text in the Sender Pool.

Are you billed for error 21703?

Billing

As of November 2026, a message that ends as Failed can incur Twilio's $0.001 fee. Check your scheduled queue so a bad service doesn't fail a whole batch.

Questions about 21703

My service has a UK number. Why can't it text the US?
Twilio doesn't use numbers from outside the US or Canada to reach mobile recipients in those countries. Add a US or Canadian sender.
I added a number just now and it's still failing. Why?
Registration or number association can still be pending. Twilio suggests sending with an explicit From for now, and retrying the service once registration finishes.
