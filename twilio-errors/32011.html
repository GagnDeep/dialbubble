Warning in calling

Twilio warning 32011: SIP infrastructure error

Twilio warning 32011 means Twilio had trouble talking to your own SIP system, because it got no reply, an error, or an invalid response. It can slow call setup or make calls fail, depending on your failover.

What does Twilio warning 32011 mean?

Twilio's name for it is Error communicating with your SIP communications infrastructure.

The problem is on your SIP endpoint or the path to it, whether that's a trunk or a <Dial><Sip>. It's aimed at people running their own PBX or SIP servers.

Twilio makes multiple delivery attempts, and each failure logs its own notification. So several warnings for a single call is normal, and each one names the SIP URI and the error response.

Most likely causes, in the order to check them

  1. A network problem between Twilio and your system.
  2. Your SIP endpoint isn't responding, because of an outage or maintenance.
  3. A firewall is blocking SIP traffic from Twilio.
  4. Your endpoint is sending an error such as SIP 500.
  5. The SIP URI in your origination settings, TwiML or API call is invalid.
  6. Your endpoint doesn't support TLSv1.2.

How to fix Twilio warning 32011

  1. Open a notification, and note the SIP URI and error response.
  2. Check that URI against your origination settings, or your <Dial><Sip> TwiML.
  3. Allow Twilio's IP ranges and ports for SIP signaling and RTP media on your firewall.
  4. Check that your SIP servers are up and handling calls from Twilio.
  5. Make sure your servers support TLSv1.2, or configure your account to allow older versions if you must.

How to prevent it

  • Configure more than one origination URI, so that failover has somewhere to go.
  • Recheck firewall rules against Twilio's published IP ranges after any network change.

Are you billed for warning 32011?

Billing

A warning has no charge of its own. Elastic SIP Trunking and SIP traffic bill at their own rates when calls connect. Check Twilio's pricing page for your setup.

Questions about 32011

Why am I getting many 32011 warnings for one call?
Twilio retries delivery, and each failed attempt raises its own notification.
Will calls still connect?
They may, with longer setup time, if a later attempt or failover works. Otherwise the call fails. It depends on your failover configuration.
