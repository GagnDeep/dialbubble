Twilio softphone mobile app: what's real

A true Twilio softphone carries the call inside the app over the internet, and Dialbubble doesn't do that. It's a remote control that has Twilio bridge your regular phone to the other person. If you need VoIP, this page explains your real options, and we'll tell you when to choose one of those instead.

How a bridge differs from a softphone

Bridge call (Dialbubble Talk)

  1. 1You tap call. Dialbubble asks Twilio to start it over the internet.
  2. 2Twilio rings your regular number, as a normal phone call.
  3. 3You answer and press any key. Twilio then dials the other person.
  4. 4You talk on your normal phone line. They see your Twilio number.

Softphone call (VoIP)

  1. 1You tap call in the app, which is signed in to Twilio with a token.
  2. 2Your voice travels over Wi-Fi or mobile data to Twilio.
  3. 3Twilio dials the other person straight away.
  4. 4You talk inside the app. They see your Twilio number.

When is a real softphone the better choice?

  • You need to answer calls to your Twilio number inside the app.
  • You often have Wi-Fi but no phone signal, or you're using a device with no phone line.
  • You make enough long calls that the extra leg adds up.
  • You don't want business calls using your mobile plan or roaming allowance.
  • A key press before each call would slow you down, as it would on a sales team.
  • You're on Android, where Dialbubble isn't available.

The bridge suits you if you make occasional calls from a Twilio number, you already have a good phone plan, and you don't want a server. See exactly how a Talk call works.

Ways to get a real softphone

Build on Twilio's Voice SDK

You have a developer and want calling in your own app.

Real in-app calls, including incoming ones. The iOS quickstart uses a backend to issue access tokens, a TwiML app, and Apple VoIP push credentials.

Register a SIP softphone to a Twilio SIP Domain

You're comfortable with SIP settings.

Twilio's SIP quickstart and softphone notes cover this: you make a SIP Domain with credentials, then enter them in a SIP app. Use a SIP Domain and not a bare Elastic SIP Trunk, because trunks don't accept softphone registration.

Use a third-party Twilio softphone

You're on Android or the web and want no code.

Nethram's Softphone for Twilio Platform says it's free, and that you pay Twilio. We haven't tested it, so read its terms before you hand over access.

Skip Twilio with a VoIP phone service

You want an app that simply works, and don't need Twilio itself.

Services like Quo, Dialpad and Grasshopper include in-app calling for a flat monthly price.

Incoming calls without a softphone

You can forward a Twilio number to your regular phone, with no app involved. Each forwarded call bills an inbound and an outbound leg. See how Twilio call forwarding works.

Softphone questions

Is Dialbubble a softphone?
No. A softphone sends the call audio over the internet through an app. On a Dialbubble Talk call, Twilio rings your regular phone number and bridges you to the other person, so the audio stays on your normal phone line.
Why doesn't Dialbubble include VoIP?
In-app calling with Twilio needs a server to issue access tokens. Dialbubble is built to have no server, so your credentials and activity stay between your iPhone and Twilio. That design rules out VoIP.
Can I use a bridge call with no cell signal?
Only if your phone can still receive a normal call, for example over your carrier's Wi-Fi Calling. A softphone only needs an internet connection.
Does a bridge call use my carrier minutes?
Usually it's treated as a normal incoming call under your plan. Check your plan, especially when you're roaming.
Which is cheaper, a softphone or a bridge?
At Twilio's US list prices in November 2026, an outbound softphone call costs about $0.018 a minute and a bridge call about $0.028. The softphone's minutes are cheaper, though building or hosting one has its own costs.
