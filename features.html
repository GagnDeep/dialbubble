What Dialbubble does

Calls, texts, recordings, usage and more, all running on your own Twilio account. Everything here is free in the app, and you can try all of it in Demo Mode.

Calling

Four ways to place a call from your Twilio number. The other person sees that number, not your personal one.

Talk
A two-way call on your regular phone. Twilio rings you first, you press any key, and only then is the other person dialed.
Voice Message
Type your message and Twilio reads it aloud when they answer, using text-to-speech. You can loop it.
Play Audio
Call someone and play an MP3 or WAV from a public link.
Custom TwiML
Write any TwiML and run it on a call, with no webhook server to host.
Call recording
Turn on recording for a call, then play it back or share it from the app.
Call history
Recents with filters, call details and your recordings in one place.

Texting

A proper inbox for your Twilio numbers, with threaded conversations for each number.

SMS and MMS conversations
Threads per number, with delivery status, a segment counter and picture viewing. MMS you send needs a public https link to the media.
Broadcast
Send one message to many recipients, with throttled sending, progress and a result for each person.
Templates
Save the messages you send often and drop them in with a tap.
Scheduled texts
Pick a send time through a Twilio Messaging Service, and cancel it later if plans change.
Pins, drafts and codes
Pin your top conversations, keep a draft in every thread, and copy sign-in codes with one tap.

Accounts and numbers

Everything you own at Twilio shows up on its own after you connect.

Multiple accounts
Add more than one Twilio account, give each its own color, and switch from Home.
Multiple numbers
Nicknames and colors, default numbers for calling and texting, and a sync with Twilio.
Per-number caller ID
Choose the number each call goes out from, including verified caller IDs.
Balance and usage
Your balance, account status, a 7-day activity chart and monthly cost by category.
Number lookup
Check a number with Twilio Lookup, plus the paid line-type and caller-name add-ons.
Global search
Find anything across your messages, calls and numbers.

Help and everyday comforts

Twilio has a lot of moving parts. Dialbubble explains them as you go.

Guided onboarding
A step-by-step setup that shows you exactly what to copy from the Twilio Console, and resumes if you leave partway.
Plain-English error guide
Every Twilio error is translated into what went wrong and how to fix it.
Demo Mode
Try every feature on simulated data. No Twilio account needed, nothing sent, nothing charged.
Face ID app lock
Lock the app behind Face ID, with a privacy screen in the app switcher.
Siri and Shortcuts
Send a text, call with a voice message or open the dialer by voice or from a Shortcut.
Contact names
Optional: see names instead of numbers everywhere. Contacts are read on your iPhone and never uploaded.
Works offline
Your latest calls and texts are saved on the iPhone, so the app opens instantly and still shows your history without a connection.
Badge and alerts
Opt in to an icon badge for unread texts and missed calls, plus best-effort alerts while the app is closed.
CSV export
Export every call and text as a spreadsheet-ready CSV file from Settings.

What Dialbubble doesn't do

Having no server keeps Dialbubble free and private. It also means a few things you should know before you install it.

No in-app calling
Dialbubble doesn't do VoIP. A Talk call rings your regular phone through a bridge, so it bills two Twilio legs.
No instant push
The app checks for new texts every 20 seconds while it's open. Alerts while it's closed are optional and best effort, because iOS decides when background checks run.
iPhone only
Dialbubble needs iOS 26 or later. There's no Android or iPad version.
A Twilio account for real use
Demo Mode works without one. Real calls and texts run on your own Twilio account.
