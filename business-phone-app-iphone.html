Best business phone app for iPhone

The best one depends on who's calling and how much. For most small teams that's Quo; for a solo owner who wants a flat bill, Grasshopper; on a tight budget in the US, Google Voice. We make Dialbubble, and it's only the best pick in one narrow case, which we explain below.

Flat plan or pay per use?

A flat plan costs the same whether you use it or not. Twilio charges for what you use, and Dialbubble's two-leg calls add up, so pay-per-use loses at higher volumes.

  • 100 minutes and 100 texts: about $7.28 a month on Twilio with Dialbubble.
  • 500 minutes and 300 texts: about $21.14 a month on Twilio with Dialbubble.
  • 1500 minutes and 500 texts: about $51.80 a month on Twilio with Dialbubble.

Compared with a $14 to $15 flat plan, Dialbubble is cheaper at light use and costs more once you pass a few hundred call minutes a month.

Estimate from Twilio's US list prices, November 2026: one local number at $1.15 a month, Talk calls billed as two legs at $0.014 a minute each, texts sent at $0.0083 plus up to $0.005 in carrier fees, and a $2.00 monthly Sole Proprietor campaign. It leaves out incoming calls and texts, recording, taxes and the one-time $19.50 registration. Sources: voice, SMS, A2P 10DLC fees. Try your own numbers in the Twilio pricing calculator.

What should a business phone app include?

  • Incoming calls that reach you, with voicemail and a greeting. This matters more than most feature lists.
  • Text registration help. In the US, business texting needs A2P 10DLC registration, however you send it.
  • A way to leave. Check that you can port your number out later.
  • Emergency calling details. Read how each app handles it, as VoIP services can differ from a mobile line.

Business phone app questions

What's the best business phone app for iPhone?
For most small teams, an app with in-app calling and a shared inbox, such as Quo, is the easiest choice. Solo owners may prefer Grasshopper's flat price, and very small US setups can start with Google Voice. Dialbubble is a niche pick, for people who already use Twilio.
Is there a free business phone app for iPhone?
Google Voice offers a free consumer number to eligible US residents. Dialbubble's app is free too, though you pay Twilio for numbers and usage. Dialpad, Quo and Grasshopper are paid, with free trials.
Can I keep my existing business number?
Quo and Twilio both support porting numbers in. Check with any provider before you cancel your old service, as the old number must stay active until the port completes.
Do these apps work with my personal iPhone?
Yes. You keep your personal number and plan, and the app adds a business number on top. Dialbubble also uses your regular phone line for Talk calls.
