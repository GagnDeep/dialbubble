Who it's for

People who have a Twilio account, or are happy to open one, and want to use it from their phone like a real phone.

  • You run a small business on a Twilio number

    Answer customers by text and call them back from your business number while you're away from your desk, without giving out your personal number.

  • You build with Twilio

    Check a number, send a test text, run some TwiML or read the real reason a message failed, all from your phone instead of the Console.

  • You want a second number without a monthly plan

    Buy a number from Twilio, connect it, and pay for what you use. Dialbubble adds nothing to the bill.

Not the right fit if you need calls inside the app, push alerts for every text, or Android. Read the limits

Up and running in three steps

  1. 1

    Get the app and connect

    Copy your Account SID and Auth Token, or an API Key, from the Twilio Console. The guided setup shows you where they are.
  2. 2

    Choose your numbers

    Dialbubble imports the numbers you own at Twilio. Pick your defaults and add your own phone number for two-way calls.
  3. 3

    Call or text

    Use your Twilio number straight away. Your first text or call is set up for you at the end of onboarding.

The two-way call, explained

A Talk call works on your regular phone, and the other person sees your Twilio number. Try it here to see the order things happen in.

  1. You tap call in Dialbubble, and Twilio rings your phone first.
  2. You answer, and a voice asks you to press any key.
  3. Only then does Twilio dial the other person.
  4. They pick up and see your Twilio number. You talk on your normal phone.

Waiting for the key press means your voicemail or call screening can't answer and leave the other person holding.

An honest note on cost

A Talk call is two Twilio calls joined together, one to you and one to them, so Twilio bills both legs at its normal rates. That is the trade-off for having no server and no VoIP.

Common questions

More answers on the support page.

Is Dialbubble really free?
Yes. The app is free, with no subscription and no markup. You pay Twilio directly for the calls, texts and numbers you use, at Twilio's normal rates.
Do I need a Twilio account?
For real calls and texts, yes. The app walks you through signing up if you don't have one. To look around first, Demo Mode works with no account at all.
Does Dialbubble make calls over the internet?
No. Dialbubble has no in-app VoIP. On a Talk call, Twilio rings your regular phone and connects you to the other person, so you talk on your normal phone line.
What number does the other person see?
The Twilio number you chose for the call, or a verified caller ID from your account. They don't see your personal number.
Why does a Talk call cost two legs?
Twilio places two calls and joins them: one to your phone and one to the other person. Twilio bills each leg at its normal rate. A Voice Message call uses one leg, because it only calls the other person.
Will I get notified about new texts?
While the app is open, new texts appear right away in an in-app banner. You can also turn on an app icon badge and alerts while the app is closed. Those use Background App Refresh, and iOS decides when checks run, so they can arrive late. Dialbubble has no server, so there are no instant push notifications.
Where are my Twilio credentials stored?
In the iPhone Keychain, on that device only. Dialbubble has no server, so we never see or receive them.
Is Dialbubble open source?
Yes. All of the code is on GitHub under the MIT license, so you can check exactly what the app does with your credentials, build it yourself, or contribute.
Is there an Android version?
No. Dialbubble is iPhone only and needs iOS 26 or later.
